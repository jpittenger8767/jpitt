Why OU? A Look at the University of Oklahoma's Meteorology Program

School Meteorology

A view of the University of Oklahoma's campus.

So, why OU?

The University of Oklahoma (OU) has long appealed to me as the place to be. The meteorology program is renowned for its excellence, and who could resist the allure of being in the heart of Tornado Alley? This combination of top notch academics with a hard to resist location made my decision to attend OU an easy one.
